# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by a Python pipeline (pandas + Jinja2), hosted on GitHub Pages, refreshed by a scheduled GitHub Action. Confirmed with the user during design; no Node toolchain, no external runtime dependencies.

## Users

The 12 managers of "The League", a Sleeper fantasy football league running since 2021. All are alumni of Downers Grove North High School. They open the site from their group chat, overwhelmingly on phones, usually mid-argument. Former managers (10-team era, 2021–2024) appear in history and may visit. The site is public but built for the league.

## Product Purpose

The definitive, auto-updating record of the league: every season, matchup, draft pick, keeper, and transaction since 2021, computed into stats the group chat actually argues about (luck, benchings, trade wins, keeper value). Success = the site is the link someone drops to settle an argument, and the Tuesday refresh becomes part of the league's weekly rhythm.

## Positioning

Sleeper's app shows the current season generically. This site holds the league's full six-season linked history with superflex- and keeper-aware analysis Sleeper doesn't compute (all-play luck, optimal-lineup efficiency, keeper rule audits, FAAB efficiency, trade retrospectives) — every number reproducible from committed data.

## Operating Context

- Weekly in-season rhythm: games end Monday night; data refreshes Tuesday morning; the group chat reacts.
- Keeper declarations and the draft dominate the offseason; the league votes on rules at the draft.
- A last-place punishment exists (details pending in `league_lore.yml`).
- The season phase drives what matters: pre-draft → draft prep; in-season → this week; offseason → the record book.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Data source: public Sleeper read API only; league ID 1315570882550202368, history walked via `previous_league_id`.
- 2021–2024 were 10-team seasons, 2025+ are 12-team; era-sensitive stats are annotated, never silently mixed.
- Superflex keeper league: QB/2RB/2WR/TE/FLEX/SUPER_FLEX/K/DEF, 2 keepers max, $125 FAAB, 6 playoff teams.
- Keeper rules (commissioner-confirmed 2026-07-31): max 2; drafted round 6+ the prior year; no back-to-back keeps; kept at the drafted round — this league has NO one-round-earlier penalty for players who left a roster; no future-year pick trades.
- Static site: no auth, no server, no comments, no external CDNs/fonts/JS libraries.
- Hand-maintained inputs: `manager_names.csv` (handle → first name; site falls back to handles), `league_lore.yml` (trophy, punishments).
- Undecided/pending: punishment history and champion notes not yet provided (names and trophy filled 2026-07-31; punishments key off the commissioner's own loser list, not derived standings).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **The League** — the deadpan-definite article is the identity; play it straight and confident.
- Alumni identity: Downers Grove North Trojans. Purple is the league's color.
- Binding assets in `images/`:
  - `Theleaguemainfinal.PNG` — primary logo: purple-caped Trojan warrior raising a beer, silver shield, purple beveled "THE LEAGUE" wordmark on black. Native to dark backgrounds.
  - `DGNLogo.jpeg` — DGN Trojans circular seal (purple/white); heritage reference, use subtly.
- Voice: playful trash talk — framing has teeth ("biggest heists", "punching-bag award"), numbers stay rigorous. Lore/superlative surfaces lean in; tables stay legible.
- Managers appear as real first names (from `manager_names.csv`), Sleeper avatars alongside.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six seasons of real Sleeper data committed as CSVs under `data/` (2021–2026; 2026 pre-draft).
- The two logo images above.
- No punishments, trophy names, or champion anecdotes are on record yet — the site must render honest placeholders, never invent lore.

## Product Principles

1. **Receipts, not vibes.** Every number on the site is reproducible from committed data; trash talk is framing, never fabrication.
2. **Built for the group chat.** Mobile-first, instant load, every page and pair-history deep-linkable.
3. **The season phase picks the front door.** Draft room pre-draft, weekly drama in season, record book in the offseason — driven by Sleeper's league status, not manual edits.
4. **History is the moat.** Six seasons, two eras, honestly annotated.
5. **Zero-maintenance record.** Auto-refresh weekly; one constant to roll the league forward each year.
