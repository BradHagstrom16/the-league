Draft & Keepers

Keeper rules

  1. A manager may keep 2 players maximum.
  2. The player must have been drafted in the previous year's draft in round 6 or later. A player may not be kept two years in a row.
  3. You keep a player at the round you drafted him.
  4. Draft pick trading is allowed before and during the draft, including keepers. Future years' picks cannot be traded.
