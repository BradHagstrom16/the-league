Draft & Keepers

Keeper rules

  1. A manager may keep 2 players maximum.
  2. The player must have been drafted in the previous year's draft in round 6 or later. A player may not be kept two years in a row.
  3. You keep a player at the round you drafted him. If two keepers were drafted in the same round, one of them moves up a round.
  4. Draft pick trading is allowed before and during the draft, including keepers. Future years' picks cannot be traded.

The audit

86 of 86 historical keepers charged correctly, the way the rule reads. Six seasons, zero disputes. The books are clean.

Round collisions

Trade for a keeper who went in the same round as one of your own and only one of them can hold that slot. The other moves up a round. These are legal, not misses.
